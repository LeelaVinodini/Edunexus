Multithreading and Object-Oriented Design

Course: Object-Oriented Programming (C++/Java) (CSE106-B)
Semester: 2, Academic Year: 1, Section: B

This unit covers multithreading and object-oriented design for Object-Oriented Programming (C++/Java).
Topics include fundamental concepts, worked examples, and practice problems.
Students are expected to understand the theoretical foundations and apply them to practical problems.
