Collections, Generics and File Input/Output

Course: Object-Oriented Programming (C++/Java) (CSE106-A)
Semester: 2, Academic Year: 1, Section: A

This unit covers collections, generics and file input/output for Object-Oriented Programming (C++/Java).
Topics include fundamental concepts, worked examples, and practice problems.
Students are expected to understand the theoretical foundations and apply them to practical problems.
